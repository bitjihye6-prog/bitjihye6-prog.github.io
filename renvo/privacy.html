Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Last updated November 2, 2026

In short: Renvo doesn’t collect, store or share your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

This policy covers the Renvo: Subscription Tracker app (“Renvo”, “the app”) and this website. Renvo was called Snip before its launch; it is the same app. If you have questions, email renvo@drakiverse.studio.

Information stored on your device

To work, Renvo saves the following only on your phone:

  • The subscriptions you add: names, prices, billing cycles, charge dates, categories, reminder settings and any cancellation links you paste
  • Your settings, such as currency and default reminder time
  • Nothing from a scanned receipt except the details you save; the photo itself is deleted right after it is read
  • Your progress on the “Find forgotten subscriptions” checklist
  • Whether you have unlocked Renvo Pro, and whether you turned the app lock on

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We don’t run any servers that receive it, and we can’t see it.

Scan a receipt

If you use Scan a receipt (part of Renvo Pro), you choose a photo or screenshot, or take a photo with your camera app. Renvo reads the text in that picture using Google ML Kit’s on-device text recognition, which runs entirely on your phone. The picture is not uploaded anywhere and is not stored: Renvo deletes its copy as soon as the text has been read. Only what you choose to save from it, such as the name, price, billing cycle and renewal date, is kept in your subscription list, on your phone. Renvo also switches off the usage reporting that comes built into the ML Kit library, so it doesn’t send Google statistics about the feature either.

The list of popular services in the add screen is built into the app. Picking one fills in its name, category and official cancellation page; nothing is looked up online.

Reminders

Renewal reminders are scheduled by your phone and shown as local notifications. They don’t go through the internet.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, your phone asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock before Renvo opens. Android does the check; Renvo never sees, receives or stores your fingerprint, face or screen-lock code. While the lock is on, Renvo is hidden in your phone’s recent-apps view and reminders don’t show subscription names.

Purchases

Renvo Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment, and we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own Renvo Pro so it can unlock the Pro features. Google’s handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Backups and exports

When you tap “Back up my list” or “Export to spreadsheet”, Renvo creates a file and opens your phone’s share menu. You choose where it goes, such as Google Drive, email or your files. Renvo doesn’t upload it anywhere by itself. Wherever you save or send it, that service’s privacy policy applies. When you restore a backup, Renvo reads only the file you pick, on your phone, checks that it is a valid Renvo backup, and leaves your current list untouched if it isn’t.

Links to other services

Some buttons open other websites or apps, such as a company’s cancellation page, your Google Play subscriptions or a Gmail search. Renvo only opens them; what happens there is covered by that service’s own privacy policy.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to show your renewal reminders.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Fingerprint / screen lock: to let you lock the app. Android handles the check.
  • Vibration: for small taps when you cut a subscription.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and restore Renvo Pro.
  • Camera or photos: only when you use Scan a receipt. Renvo doesn’t ask for camera or photo-library permission: your camera app or Android’s photo picker hands Renvo just the one picture you choose.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect personal information or create user accounts.
  • We don’t use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don’t sell, rent or share any information.
  • We don’t ask for bank, card or email login details.

Deleting your information

You’re always in control. Delete any subscription inside the app, or uninstall Renvo to remove everything it stored. Because we keep no copies, there’s nothing else to delete.

If you email us

If you write to renvo@drakiverse.studio, we receive your email address and whatever you put in your message. We use it only to reply to you. It's kept in our email inbox, and we'll delete it if you ask.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR, and California's CCPA/CPRA give you rights to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your personal information, and to object to how it's used. Because Renvo keeps your information only on your phone, you can see, change, export or delete all of it yourself, right in the app. The only personal information we might hold is an email you sent us; ask and we'll show it to you or delete it. We don't sell or share personal information, so there's nothing to opt out of. If you're not happy with how we handled a privacy request, you can contact your local privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Renvo is meant for adults managing their own subscriptions and isn’t directed at children under 13.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking. It loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers, which may receive your IP address. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll post the new version on this page and update the date above.
