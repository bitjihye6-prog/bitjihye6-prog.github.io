Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version: Lapse has no accounts and no servers. Everything you add (items, bills, dates, people, photos and notes) stays on your phone. We never see it, sell it or share it.

Who we are

Lapse is made by Drakiverse Studio, an independent app studio in Canada. You can reach us at lapse@drakiverse.studio.

Information stored on your phone

The things you add to Lapse (names, expiry and due dates, bill amounts, people's names, notes, links and photos) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Lapse does not send this information to us or to anyone else.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. No reminder data leaves your device.

Voice add

If you use the microphone button, your phone's built-in speech recognition turns what you say into text. Depending on your phone's settings, Google's speech service may process the audio to do this. Lapse only receives the resulting text and does not store any recordings.

Purchases

Lapse Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns whether you own Lapse Pro.

Backups and exports

Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup), so your items come back if you change phones. This is controlled in your phone's settings and the backup belongs to your Google account. If you use Export, Lapse creates a file and hands it to the app you choose; what happens next is up to you.

Calendar, sharing and links

If you choose to add an item to Google Calendar, share a reminder, or open a renewal website, Lapse opens that app or page with the details you chose to send. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications and exact alarms: to show your reminders on time.
  • Run at startup: to put reminders back after your phone restarts.
  • Microphone: only when you tap the mic to add something by voice.
  • Camera or photos: only when you choose to attach a photo to an item.

What we don't do

  • No ads tracking, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No bank or account logins.

Deleting your information

Delete any item in the app, or uninstall Lapse to remove everything from your phone. You can also delete Lapse's backup from your Google account settings.

Children

Lapse is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
