From job ad to PDF in a few taps

Upload your resume once. After that, every job you want gets its own version.

  1. Add your resume oncePDF, Word, photos of each page, screenshots, or type it in. Fix anything Drakio misread.
  2. Add the job adSnap a photo, pick screenshots, paste the text, share it from Indeed, LinkedIn or Chrome, scan a QR code on a “Now hiring” sign, or open a job link.
  3. Drakio reads the adJob title, company, country, the skills it asks for and what to check before you apply.
  4. Fill the gaps honestlyOne yes/no question for each requirement your resume doesn't show. Only a “yes” is added.
  5. See what changedEvery change as Before → After with the reason. Keep it, undo it or rewrite it in your own words.
  6. Save the PDF and track the jobHiring-software friendly, then each job keeps its own resume, status and follow-up reminder.

Honest by design

A tailored resume should still be your resume. Drakio never makes things up.

Never invents

Only what's true

Every added line comes from your resume or a “yes” you gave. New skills you type get an “Is this true?” check.

Nothing hidden

Every change shown

Nothing reaches the PDF until you've seen it in “What changed”, with the reason for each edit.

Your match

Matched, partly, missing

See how your resume lines up with the ad, plus what to check before you apply.

ATS friendly

Built to be read

One column, real text, standard headings, one date style, black and white. Six clean fonts, 10 to 12 pt.

No extras

No photo, no ID numbers

Drakio never adds a photo, ID numbers, religion or ethnicity. Personal details are added only if the ad asks and you agree.

Follow up

Track every job

A status and a follow-up reminder for each job. Reminders never name the job or employer.
