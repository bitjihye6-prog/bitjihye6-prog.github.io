drakio privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The short version

Drakio has no servers and no accounts. Your resume, the job ads you add and the resumes Drakio makes stay on your phone. They leave it only when you save or share a file yourself. Drakio goes online only to open a job link you choose, and it never sends your resume anywhere.

Who we are

Drakio is made by Drakiverse Studio, an independent app developer based in Canada.

What stays on your phone

Your resume details (name, contact details, links you choose to add, work history, education, skills and certificates), the job ads you add, your answers to Drakio's questions, the tailored resumes it makes, your job list, statuses, follow-up days and times, any follow-up notes you write and any interview times you add are saved only in Drakio's private storage on your device. Drakio has no server to send them to, and it never sends them anywhere.

Reading resumes, photos and QR codes

When you add your resume as a PDF, Word file or photo, or add a job ad from a photo, a screenshot or a "Now hiring" QR code, Google ML Kit reads the text or code on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. If Android closes Drakio while the camera is open (this can happen on phones with little memory), Drakio picks up the photo you took from Android when it opens again, so you don't have to take it twice. Drakio keeps only the text it read and the details you save, and deletes its temporary copy of each file or photo straight after reading it. ML Kit's usage reporting to Google is switched off in Drakio. Files you pick are treated as untrusted: Drakio checks their type and size first and never runs anything inside them.

Job links

When you paste a job link, type one in, share one to Drakio, or scan a QR code that holds one, Drakio first shows a "Job link found" box with the website's name on its own line, and the full link if you want to see it. Nothing opens until you tap "Open and read the job". Drakio then opens that job page inside the app, the same way a web browser would. Only secure (https) pages open: a link that starts with http:// is opened as https:// instead. While the page loads, Drakio checks inside the page whether it looks like a job ad, so the "Read this job" button only turns on when there's an ad to read; that check gives Drakio only a yes or no. When you tap "Read this job", Drakio copies the text on the page into the app and reads it on your phone.

Opening a page means your phone connects to that job website, which sees what any website sees when you visit it, such as your IP address, and may set its own cookies or store data in the page viewer. Drakio sends nothing to the website: not your resume, your details or anything else from the app. What happens on that website is covered by that website's own policy. When you go to apply, sign in, upload a file or leave for another website, Drakio offers to open the page in your own browser instead, and you can always do that from the job page. "Delete all my data" also clears the cookies, saved pages and website data these pages leave in Drakio.

Sharing a job ad to Drakio

You can share a job ad's text or link to Drakio from another app, such as Indeed, LinkedIn or Chrome. Android hands the shared text to Drakio on your phone. Drakio limits its length, treats it as plain text and never runs anything in it. A link goes to the "Job link found" box described above; other text goes into the job ad box for you to check. Nothing is uploaded.

Saving and sharing your resume

When you tap Save to phone, Drakio saves the PDF in your phone's Downloads folder (on Android 9 and older, in the folder you pick). Saving there needs no storage permission, and Drakio can only see the files it saved itself. When you tap Share, the PDF goes only where you choose, such as an email or a job website. Drakio clears its own temporary copies afterwards.

What Drakio never adds

Drakio uses only what's on your resume or what you confirm, and shows every added line in "What changed" before it goes into your PDF. It never adds a photo, ID numbers, religion, ethnicity or state of origin. If your own resume already lists personal details such as date of birth, age, religion, marital status, nationality, gender, health or ID numbers, Drakio leaves them out of the new resume and shows them to you under "Text Drakio couldn't place" instead.

Reminders

If you set a follow-up day and time, Drakio shows a reminder on your phone then. The reminder says only "Time to follow up" and never shows the job, the employer or your follow-up note. The note shows only on the job's page inside Drakio.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, your phone checks your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android does the check, and Drakio never sees or stores your fingerprint or face. While the lock is on, Drakio also hides its preview in your recent apps and Android blocks screenshots of Drakio.

Backups

Drakio's data is left out of Android's Google account backup, so your resume isn't copied to the cloud. This also means that if you uninstall Drakio or change phones, your saved resume and jobs are not restored. Keep your own copy of any PDF you need.

Permissions the app uses

Internet, only to open job links you choose. Notifications, for follow-up reminders. Run at startup, to put your reminders back after your phone restarts. Vibration, when a reminder arrives. Network state, so the job page viewer can tell you when you're offline. Fingerprint, face or screen lock, only if you turn on the app lock. Photos and files are picked through Android's own picker, so Drakio doesn't need access to your whole photo library or storage.

Purchases

If Drakio Pro is offered, it will be sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment, and we never see your card details.

What we don't do

No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no crash reporting. We don't sell or share personal information, because we never receive it.

Not advice

Drakio helps you organise your resume and job applications. It doesn't give legal, immigration, financial or career advice, and it doesn't apply for jobs or contact employers for you.

Deleting your information

Delete any job by swiping it or from its menu, or use "Delete all my data" in the app to erase your resume, jobs, reminders and the job page viewer's cookies, saved pages and website data at once. The count of free resumes you've used is kept, so deleting doesn't reset it. Uninstalling Drakio also removes everything from your phone. PDFs you saved or shared are yours to delete wherever you kept them.

If you email us

If you write to hello@drakiverse.studio, we receive your email address and whatever you put in your message. We use it only to reply to you. It's kept in our email inbox, and we'll delete it if you ask.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR, and California's CCPA/CPRA give you rights to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your personal information, and to object to how it's used. Because Drakio keeps your information only on your phone, you can see, change, export or delete all of it yourself, right in the app. The only personal information we might hold is an email you sent us; ask and we'll show it to you or delete it. If you're not happy with how we handled a privacy request, you can contact your local privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Drakio is a general-audience tool for people looking for work. It isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

This website

This page has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
