folderoo privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version

Folderoo does not collect, store, sell or see your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your claims, timeline entries, deadlines, diary check-ins, documents, photos and expenses are saved only in Folderoo's private storage on your device. Drakiverse Studio has no servers and no access to this information.

Backups

Android may include Folderoo's data in your own Google account backup, which is controlled by you in your phone's settings. Folderoo Pro can also create an encrypted backup file protected by a password you choose; you decide where to save it. We never receive the file or the password.

Sharing

When you export a PDF summary or share a document, it leaves your phone only through the share option you pick, and only to the people or apps you choose.

Purchases

Folderoo Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details.

Permissions

Notifications and alarms are used for your reminders. Fingerprint or screen lock is used only to unlock the app. The camera and photos are used only when you add a document.

Not advice

Folderoo helps you remember and organize your claim. It does not provide legal, medical, or insurance advice and is not connected to any insurer.
