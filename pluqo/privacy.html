Pluqo privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The short version

Pluqo does not collect, store, sell or see your data. The camera is used only to see your hands, and the picture never leaves your phone.

The camera

Pluqo uses your phone's camera so you can play instruments in the air. Hand tracking (MediaPipe) runs on your phone, inside the app. The camera picture and the positions of your hands are used only while you play and are never saved, uploaded or sent anywhere. Pluqo never uses the microphone.

No internet

Pluqo has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet. Drakiverse Studio has no servers that receive anything from the app.

Videos you record

With Pluqo Pro you can record a video of yourself playing. A video is only made when you tap Record. It's kept in the app's temporary folder until you choose Save to Gallery or Share; it leaves your phone only through the share option you pick, and only to the people or apps you choose.

Your songs (DJ)

In DJ mode you can add songs from your phone. You pick them yourself in Android's file picker, so Pluqo needs no storage or music permission and can't see any other files. Pluqo copies the songs you pick into its own private folder on your phone so it can play them. They're never uploaded or shared, and you can remove them in the app at any time. Uninstalling Pluqo removes them too.

What Pluqo keeps

Pluqo keeps three small settings on your phone: whether you have Pro, whether you've seen the setup tips, and the last instrument you played, plus any songs you added for DJ mode. It doesn't keep your strings, loops or anything you play after you leave the play screen.

Purchases

Pluqo Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details. Pluqo only learns whether Pro was bought.

Permissions

Camera: to see your hands so you can play. That's the only permission Pluqo asks for. Songs for DJ mode come from Android's file picker, which needs no permission.

Deleting your information

Remove DJ songs in the app with the ✕ next to each song. Uninstalling Pluqo removes its settings and songs from your phone. Videos you saved to your Gallery or shared are yours to delete wherever you kept them.

If you email us

If you write to hello@drakiverse.studio, we receive your email address and whatever you put in your message. We use it only to reply to you. It's kept in our email inbox, and we'll delete it if you ask.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR, and California's CCPA/CPRA give you rights to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your personal information, and to object to how it's used. Pluqo doesn't collect personal information, so there's nothing for us to show or delete except an email you sent us; ask and we'll show it to you or delete it. We don't sell or share personal information. If you're not happy with how we handled a privacy request, you can contact your local privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Pluqo isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect information from anyone.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It's hosted on Netlify, which may keep standard server logs, and it loads typefaces from Google Fonts on some pages, so Google may receive your IP address.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
