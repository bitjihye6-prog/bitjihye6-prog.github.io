Pro & payments

What does “one-time purchase” mean?

You pay once through Google Play and Pro unlocks the Pro features the app has today. There's no subscription, nothing renews, and you're never charged again for it.

We don't sell Pro with “coming soon” promises: what's listed on the Pro screen is what you get.

How much is Pro?

Each app has its own Pro. Google Play shows the exact price in your currency before you buy. Every app is free to download and has a free plan.

I got a new phone or reinstalled. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you bought Pro with, then open the app. Pro comes back on its own.

In Lastly, Folderoo and Drakio you can also open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases go through Google Play, so refunds do too. Within 48 hours of buying, you can request one yourself from your Google Play order history. After that, email the app's address below and we'll help.

Do you ever see my card details?

No. Google Play handles the payment. The app only learns whether Pro was bought.

Your data

Do I need an account?

No. None of our apps have accounts, logins or sign-ups.

Where is my information stored?

On your phone. Our apps have no servers, so your subscriptions, bills, claims and resumes aren't sent to us or anyone else. Data only leaves your phone when you choose to share, export or back it up.

Each app's privacy policy has the details: Renvo · Lastly · Folderoo · Drakio.

Can you recover my data if I lose my phone?

We can't, because we never have a copy. Where the app has a backup or export option, use it to keep your own copy somewhere safe.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling an app deletes its data from your phone. Lastly, Folderoo and Drakio also have a one-tap option to delete everything.

Do the apps give advice?

No. Our apps organize and remind. They don't give legal, medical, financial, insurance or career advice.

Bugs & ideas

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Email the app's address and start the subject with Bug: or Idea: so we can sort them. For a bug, say what you tapped, what happened, and your phone model.

We read every message and usually reply within 2 business days.
