airlo

Play music
in thin air

Your camera follows your hands, and you play instruments in the air: draw glowing harp strings, strum a guitar, tap piano keys, hit drums or bow a cello.

  • Harp
  • Guitar
  • Piano
  • Drums
  • Cello

Works best with both hands in view, good light, and sound on. Prop your phone up, or use a laptop.

Private by design: hand tracking runs in your browser and the camera picture never leaves your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored. Recordings are only made when you tap Record and go straight to your Downloads.

Opened from Instagram, TikTok or another app? Tap ⋯ → Open in browser so the camera works.
